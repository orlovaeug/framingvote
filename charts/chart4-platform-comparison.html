TikTok Strategy
Invest in authentic creator relationships. Speed and cultural fluency outperform budget. The algorithm rewards novelty — a first-time candidate can outperform a 20-year incumbent with one viral video.
Instagram Strategy
Build on existing networks. Use Reels to reinforce identity among known supporters. Instagram converts and consolidates; it rarely surprises. Best for fundraising calls and GOTV among the already-committed.
Source: Research analysis of 800K+ Instagram posts and 1.1M+ TikTok posts from the 2024 presidential election cycle.
